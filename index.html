Jumping Daniel, a four-legged walking robot

Designed, 3D-printed, wired and programmed by one student. On 11 November 2026 it trotted 22.9 cm in 27.92 s with no cable, its Pi riding on a power bank.

Two views of the robot's measured trot

At 17:22, still on a cable, JD trotted four cycles beside a tape measure. Diagonal legs swing together: front left with rear right, then front right with rear left.

It walks without a cable

For the 19:10 run the Raspberry Pi ran on a 5 V USB power bank riding on the robot, and the eight servos on a 3S 2200 mAh LiPo zip-tied under the chassis.

See the 19:10 run on video

Printed robot parts

Body, legs and head are 3D-printed. Each leg has a hip that turns and a knee that lifts, both Hiwonder HTD-35H servos, and the BusLinker V2.5 board sits in its own printed holder.

How the robot is wired

robot.py and gait.py run on the Raspberry Pi and send each joint its next angle through the BusLinker V2.5, which talks to all eight servos on one serial bus.

What the robot's walking code does

The trot walks today. The creep, one leg at a time, can't yet lift two of its feet on the floor: the robot rests on one diagonal. See the wiring
